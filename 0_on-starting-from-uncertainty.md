# On starting from uncertainty

*I am not entirely sure where this research begins.*

For a long time I thought I was interested in materials.Cement,plastic,metal,broken objects,things found on the street.Looking back,however,I think materials were never really the point.

What kept returning was a certain form of instability.

I noticed it in the objects I was collecting,in the spaces I was drawn to,and eventually in the works themselves.I became interested in situations that seemed unable to fully settle into a stable condition:things suspended between construction and collapse,between living and non-living,between control and unpredictability.

This *diary* is an attempt to understand why.
Trying to understand why.

However I feel lighter writing down my rabbit hole of thoughts.I feel like every time I do so I am metaphorically taking away some clothes from you know,the well known *"chair-wardrobe"*.The one covered in clothes,so many that you cannot even see the chair underneath.While I'm writing these exact words the pile of random clothes on it gets smaller.And eventually I am gonna be able to see the structure,the skeleton,the chair.Or well I hope so.More likely as I take away one t-shirt,a dress is going to take its place.

I am aware that,just as the clothes will probably never fully disappear from the chair-wardrobe,my questions will probably never be fully answered.That's the beauty of it.

Truth to be told these questions were originally conceived as the beginning of a possible thesis,but I am publishing it here because the questions it contains extend beyond that context.I feel they belong to a broader research process that continues to evolve through making,reading,collecting and observing.
And the more I know the more I see the questions getting bigger and bigger.

I do not see the following chapters as a finished argument of course.Rather,they document a series of attempts to approach a set of recurring questions that continue to shape my practice and my identity as well.

Why am I repeatedly drawn to unstable systems?What makes certain materials appear alive while others seem inert?And what happens when those distinctions begin to break down?

Just to name a few.The pages that follow do not offer definitive answers as I said.
They simply mark a point within an ongoing investigation.

*And that sounds like a good start.*

Talk to you soon

*Mia*
